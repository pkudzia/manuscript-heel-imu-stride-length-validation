Figure 1. Experimental setup and gyroscope processing. (a) Filtered sagittal-plane signals from the left (black) and right (blue) heel IMUs during one out-and-back trial. Labels mark activity phases, red bars mark off-mat intervals, and the box is expanded in panel c. (b) Heel IMUs clipped to the posterolateral shoe using 3D-printed housings during GaitRite walking. (c) Mean gyroscope signal over a heel-strike-defined gait cycle, pooled across participants and conditions. Shading shows ±1 SD, dashed lines mark heel strike and toe off, and blue marks swing.

Figure 2. Stride length agreement between IMU and GaitRite. (a) All 10,472 matched strides. The dashed line marks identity, and the contour encloses the densest 97.5% of points. Linear fit R² = 0.954, slope = 1.012, and ICC(2,1) = 0.976. (b) Bland–Altman plot. The red line marks mean bias (+0.003 m), dashed lines mark the 95% limits of agreement (−0.073 to +0.078 m), and the dotted line shows proportional bias (slope = 0.036).

Figure 3. Participant stride-length accuracy across conditions (n = 32). Dots show participants sorted by MAE with 95% CIs. The solid red and dashed grey lines mark the mean (2.5 cm) and median (2.4 cm), respectively.
